## who am i

My full name is Tran Manh Duy. Most people call me Tran Duy, or Tranduy1dol. The nickname came from a friend after he saw me dance like Kha Banh, who was very famous at the time (2023, if I remember right). You can call me Tran Duy, or Duy Tran. Please don't call me by my full name.

## what i do

I'm currently a software engineer at Coc Coc. Before that, I spent about two or three years at Sotatek, moving from researcher to backend developer. My stack is fairly mixed: Go, Rust, and C++, plus tools like PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Docker, Kafka, and so on.

I just graduated from UET-VNU, majoring in Information Technology (Japanese Market Oriented). I don't have much to say about university. Before that, I went to a regular high school in Hai Phong. I won a few competitive programming prizes — a skill I had almost forgotten, though I'm sharpening it again now. I started programming at 14, when I got a chance to join a contest and learn Pascal.

Besides programming, I spend most of my time reading and taking notes. I didn't take notes before this year because I didn't see the point. After learning a few techniques, I started — mainly to keep what I know, and to share it.

Other things I'm interested in:

- Music: I have a guitar and a violin, but I'm still a beginner. I need more time to practice.
- Art: I like it as a way to express how I see the world, but I'm pretty bad at drawing.
- Psychology: For me, this is a way to understand how people think and act. I've finished two books on it: *Introduction to Lacan's Psychoanalysis*, and another that introduces the basic concepts.
- Philosophy: Most of my interest is in Stoicism. It gives me a way to act, and a way to think about almost anything.
